# Trailer-capture checklist

- [ ] Record the exact source SHA, UE5.1 version, platform, build type, and
  settings before capture.
- [ ] Capture splash/main menu and the Black Meridian title card.
- [ ] Show insertion movement, camera bob reduction setting, crouch/lean/
  mantle/slide, and the first ARX-4 engagement.
- [ ] Show a headshot, hit marker, reload, weapon switch, cover penetration,
  directional damage, and squad assist.
- [ ] Show the optional archive interaction and the seawall escalation.
- [ ] Show extraction/final defense and the ending subtitle without claiming
  authored cinematic assets that are not present.
- [ ] Show Signal Control node rotation, team score, scoreboard, and results.
- [ ] Include one server/client authority clip or an on-screen evidence card.
- [ ] Capture at 1080p with frame-time overlay off in the final edit and retain
  a raw performance capture separately.
- [ ] Review every shot for copyrighted names, logos, sounds, models, or
  unlicensed external assets before publishing.
